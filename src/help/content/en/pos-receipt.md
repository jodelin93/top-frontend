After **Complete sale**, the **Sale complete** window opens. At the top, the **Give back** box reminds you of the change to give.


You can then:

- **Print receipt**: prints on the connected receipt printer (see [Hardware](topic:hardware)), otherwise through the browser's print window.
- **Email receipt**: the customer's address is suggested. Check "The customer asked to receive this receipt by e-mail (no marketing)", then click **Send**.
- **Receipt link (SMS, WhatsApp)**: click **Create link**, then **Copy link** or **Share**. The link is valid for 30 days.
- **New sale**: closes the window and clears the screen for the next customer.

> **Tip:** a reprint is always marked COPY. Past sales are reprinted from **Admin** > **Sales** (see [Sales history](topic:sales-history)).

> **Warning:** sending by email is only possible if email is set up for the store, and it is limited to a few sends per minute. It does not count as consent to receive advertising.

If the store has checked **Print automatically when a sale is completed** (see [Receipts and printing](topic:settings-receipts)), the receipt prints without a click.
