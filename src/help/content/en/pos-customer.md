With no customer, the sale goes to the **Walk-in customer**. Choose a customer to give them loyalty points, apply their group prices, use their store credit or sell on credit.

1. Click **Walk-in customer** at the top of the cart (**F4** key).
2. Type at least 2 characters: name, phone, email or customer code.
3. Click the right customer in the list. Their points are shown, for example **4037 pts**.

![The Customer window](shot:pos-customer-dialog)

To create a new customer:

1. In the same window, click **New customer**.
2. Fill in **First name**, **Last name**, **Phone** and, if possible, **Email**.
3. Click **Add customer**. They are chosen for the sale right away.

If the customer belongs to a group (for example Wholesale), their group prices and group discount apply right away (see [Group prices and discount](topic:pos-group-pricing)). Removing the customer brings back the normal prices.

> **Tip:** for a customer who has an account, the window also offers **Take a payment**: you record a payment on their account. Cash goes into your till shift. A large payment by another method (bank transfer, check) needs a manager's approval.

> **Warning:** searching for and creating customers needs a connection. Offline, the sale continues as a walk-in customer.

## Choose the salesperson

The salesperson is the person who helped the customer; they get credit for the sale in reports (commissions). They can be different from the cashier.

1. Under the customer, open the **No salesperson** list.
2. Choose the name. It is printed on the receipt.
