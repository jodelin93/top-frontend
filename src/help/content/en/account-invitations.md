## Accept or decline an invitation

When another store adds your email address and you already have an account, you receive an invitation. It appears in **Store invitations** (on the **Account security** page) and at the top of the **Dashboard**, with the store name and the role offered.

1. Read the store name and the **Role** offered.
2. Click **Accept** to work in this store, or **Decline** if you don't know it.

You keep your password: the store that invites you does not know it.

> **Tip:** if two-factor authentication is required for you, turn it on first: the invitations then appear.

## Switch stores

If your account belongs to several stores, the **Stores** section appears on **Account security**: "Your account belongs to several stores. Choose the one to work in."

1. Open **Account security**.
2. In **Stores**, the active store is marked **Current**.
3. Click **Switch** next to the other store.
4. The app reloads your permissions for that store and opens the till or the admin area.

> **Tip:** to change branch or register within the same store, use the register list at the top of the till screen instead.
