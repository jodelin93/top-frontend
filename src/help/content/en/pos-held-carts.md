A customer forgot something? Set their cart aside and serve the next customer.

1. Click **Hold** (key **F6**).
2. The message "Cart held as H-000003. Resume it from Held carts (F9)." appears and the cart is emptied.
3. To resume it, click **Held carts** in the top bar (key **F9**; on a phone: **⋯** then **Held carts**).
4. Choose the **This register** or **Whole store** tab.
5. Click **Resume** next to the right cart.

![The list of held carts](shot:pos-held-carts)

The resumed cart shows "Resumed held cart — completing it closes the hold." Finish the sale as usual.

> **Warning:** items in a held cart stay reserved. The cart expires after a set time (24 hours, for example). Holding a cart requires a connection.

> **Tip:** if prices have changed since the cart was held, the "Prices changed since this cart was held" window shows the old and new totals and asks you to confirm. The trash can in the list cancels a held cart: its items go back on sale.
