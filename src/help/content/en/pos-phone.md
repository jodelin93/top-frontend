On a phone (or any narrow screen), the till adapts: products and the cart become two tabs, and the secondary buttons move into a menu.

![The till on a phone, Products tab](shot:pos-phone-products)

## The two tabs

- **Products**: search, categories and product tiles. Tap a tile to add it to the cart. A short "Added: …" message confirms it.
- **Cart (3)**: the number in parentheses is the number of items. Here you find the customer, the lines, the discounts and the totals.

## The Charge bar

At the bottom of the **Products** tab, a bar always stays visible: it shows the number of items and the total. Tap **Charge** to go straight to payment without switching tabs. Tap the summary to see the cart.

## The ⋯ menu (More actions)

Tap **⋯** at the top right for the top-bar actions that do not fit:

![The More actions menu](shot:pos-phone-menu)

- **Open drawer** and **Clock in / Clock out**;
- **Held carts** and **Returns**;
- **Admin** (depending on your role);
- **Help**;
- **Account security** and **Language**;
- **Sign out**.

The shift and the register list stay on the second line of the top bar.

> **Tip:** on a phone, the cursor does not go back to the search box by itself after each item is added (so the keyboard does not keep popping up). Tap the search box to type or scan.

> **Tip:** windows (payment, customer, discounts…) open full screen; their main buttons stay at the bottom, within thumb reach.
