1. Open the **Loyalty** tab.
2. Check **Run a loyalty programme**.
3. Set **Points earned (% of each sale)**, the **Value of one point**, the **Minimum points to spend at once** and the **Max share of a sale paid with points**.
4. Read the **Example** at the bottom: it updates with your numbers.
5. Click **Save**.

![The Loyalty tab](shot:settings-loyalty)

> **Tip:** if you turn off the program, customers keep their points; they simply can no longer earn or use them.
