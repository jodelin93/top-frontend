The customer display is a second screen facing the customer. It shows the items, the total, then the change given. It runs on the same computer as the till, without going through the Internet.

1. Plug a second screen into the till computer.
2. In **Admin** > **Hardware**, click **Open customer display**. You can also open a new window of the same browser at the app's address followed by /customer-display.
3. Drag this window onto the customer's screen and make it full screen (F11 key).
4. Go back to the till window and sell as usual.

![The customer display waiting for a sale](shot:customer-display)

With no sale in progress, the screen shows the store name and **Welcome!**. During the sale, it shows each item with its quantity and price, then discounts, tax and the **TOTAL**. At the end, it shows **Thank you!**, the total paid and the change.

> **Warning:** open the customer display in the same browser and the same session as the till. Another machine or another browser shows nothing.

> **Tip:** the customer display keeps working offline, because it does not go through the Internet. The F1 key does not open help there: the screen is meant for the customer.
