The manual is built into the app. It opens on the topic that matches what you are doing.

## Open help

- Press the **F1** key, anywhere in the app.
- At the till: click the **?** icon at the top of the screen. On a phone: **⋯** then **Help**.
- In the admin area: click **?** at the top of the menu, or **Help** at the bottom of the menu. On a phone: **?** in the top bar.
- On the login page: click **Need help?**.

Help opens in a panel on the right (full screen on a phone). If a window is open, for example **Payment**, help shows the topic for that window.

![The help panel open at the till](shot:help-drawer)

## Search

1. Type one or more words in **Search the help...**, for example "change" or "transfer".
2. Matching topics appear with an excerpt; the words found are highlighted.
3. Click a topic to read it.

Search ignores accents and capitals ("etiquette" finds "étiquette") and tolerates a small typo.

## Read a topic

- Click an image to enlarge it; click again (or press Esc) to close it.
- **Related topics**, at the bottom, suggests similar topics.
- **Back** returns to the previous topic or to the help home.
- **Full page** opens the help center in the page, at the address /help.

Help follows the app's language. If a topic is not translated yet, it appears in English (or French) with a message.
