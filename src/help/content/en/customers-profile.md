The profile has five tabs: **Details**, **Account** (depending on your permissions), **Addresses & contacts**, **Notes** and **Activity**.

![A customer's profile](shot:customer-detail)

- **Details**: contact info, balance, credit limit, group, marketing consent, loyalty points and **Recent purchases**.
- **Account**: the [credit account](topic:customers-accounts).
- **Addresses & contacts**: **Billing** or **Shipping** addresses and people to contact (useful for a business).
- **Activity**: the full history (sales, payments, points, notes).

> **Tip:** the date of birth, tax number and address are visible only to people who manage customers or can see balances.

## Add an internal note

1. Open the **Notes** tab.
2. Write the note. It is never shown to the customer.
3. Choose who can see it: **Everyone who can see customers** or **Managers only**.
4. Click **Add note**.

## Custom fields

The **Custom fields** tab of the **Customers** page adds your own information about customers (preferred contact, size…):

1. Click **New field**.
2. Enter the **Label** (the **Key** is created automatically if you leave it empty) and choose the **Type**. For a list, enter the **Options** separated by commas.
3. Check **Required** if needed and set the **Order**.
4. Click **Create field**. It appears on each customer's form and profile; set it to **Hidden** to hide it without deleting it.
