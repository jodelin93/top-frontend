The **Reports** page (**Sales** menu) is only for people allowed to see reports.

![The Sales by day report](shot:admin-reports)

| Topic | Reports |
| --- | --- |
| Sales | By day, by product, by category, by cashier, by hour, by salesperson |
| Payments and taxes | Payments by method, by currency, tax summary, discounts given |
| Returns and voids | Returns and refunds, voided sales |
| Inventory | Inventory valuation, low stock, stock card, counts and adjustments, transfers |
| Cash | Shifts and cash variances |
| Purchasing | Purchases, supplier aging |
| Expenses | Expenses |
| Customers | Loyalty points |
| Administration | Audit log activity |

1. Click the report you want in the list on the left (on a phone: in the **Report** list at the top).
2. Choose the date range: **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **This month**, or change the dates directly. The start date cannot be after the end date.
3. If needed, choose a branch in **All branches**.
4. The table shows with a **Total** row. **Generated …** shows when it was calculated.

Some reports show the current state (**Inventory valuation**, **Low stock**): the date picker disappears. Supplier aging and audit log activity cover the whole store (branch filter grayed out). For the **Stock card**, choose an item first.

> **Tip:** each amount stays in its own currency: currencies are never added together.

> **Warning:** cost, margin and cost-value columns only appear if your role can see costs, including in exports. An account limited to certain branches only sees their figures.

## Save filters

1. Set the date range, the branch and the parameters.
2. Click **Save filters**, type a **Filter name**, check **Share with the team** if needed, then click **Save**.
3. To reuse it, choose it in **Saved filters...**.

A relative date range like "Last 7 days" stays relative. To delete one of your filters, choose it, then click the trash can.
