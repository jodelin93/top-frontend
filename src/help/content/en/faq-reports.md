## A report doesn't show cost or margin

These columns require the permission to view costs. The owner, administrator, and manager have it by default; an accountant does not, unless it is added in [Roles and permissions](topic:roles-permissions).

## An export's download link no longer works

Each link is valid for 15 minutes. Go back to **Reports**, **Background exports** area, and click **Download** again. If the export is more than 24 hours old, it is **Expired**: run it again.

## The report shows only part of the sales

- Check the selected period and branch.
- An account limited to certain branches sees only their figures.
- The business day can start after midnight (see [Business day](topic:shifts-settings)): a sale at 2 a.m. can count for the previous day.
