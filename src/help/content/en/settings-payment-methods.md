1. Open the **Payment methods** tab.
2. Click **New payment method**, or the pencil of an existing method.
3. Choose the type: cash, card, mobile payment (MonCash), bank transfer, store credit…
4. Say whether the method **Opens the cash drawer** and whether it **Requires a reference** (a MonCash transaction number, for example).
5. Save.

![The Payment methods tab](shot:settings-payment-methods)

> **Tip:** an inactive method disappears from the till but stays in the sales history. The name shown at the till is the one entered here.
