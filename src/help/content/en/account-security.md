The **Account security** page holds your personal settings. Everyone can open it:

- at the till: the shield icon at the top right (on a phone: **⋯** then **Account security**);
- in admin: **Account security** at the bottom of the menu.

It contains: **Two-factor authentication**, **Store invitations** (if you have any), **Stores** (if your account has several), **Change password** and **Where you're signed in**.

![The Account security page](shot:account-security)

## Change your password

1. In **Current password**, type your current password.
2. In **New password**, type the new one (at least 8 characters).
3. Type it again in **Confirm password**.
4. Click **Change password**.

You stay signed in on this device; all your other devices are signed out.

> **Warning:** the old password is always required. If you forgot it, ask an administrator to reset it (see [Account locked or forgotten password](topic:faq-account)).

> **Tip:** choose a phrase that is easy to remember but hard to guess (three words and a number). Never write your password down near the till.

## See your sessions and sign out other devices

**Where you're signed in** lists each device where your account is open: browser, system, sign-in date and last activity. The current device is marked **This device**.

1. Look for a device you don't recognize.
2. Click **Sign out this session** on its row.
3. To close everything except the current device, click **Sign out everywhere else** (the number shows how many sessions will be closed).

> **Tip:** do this if you forgot to sign out on a shared PC or after losing a phone, and change your password too.
