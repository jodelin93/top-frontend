The bell flags important events: low stock, purchase orders and expenses to approve, till, payments, devices, system. The blue number shows how many alerts are unread.

1. Click the bell (at the top of the till or of the admin menu).
2. Read the list of recent alerts. The type appears in color, for example **LOW STOCK**.
3. Click an alert to open what it is about, and mark it as read: a **Low stock** alert for one product opens the **Inventory** on that product and its location; a low-stock summary for a location opens that location with **Low stock only**; stock counts to approve open the **Counts** tab; the other alerts open their page (expenses, purchasing, shifts, payments, devices, system).
4. Click **Dismiss** next to an alert to mark it as read without opening it, or click **Mark all as read**.
5. Click **All notifications and preferences** to open the full page.

![The Notifications page and preferences](shot:notifications-page)

On the **Notifications** page:

1. Choose **Unread** or **All** in the list at the top.
2. Click **Mark as read** on an alert, or **Mark all as read**.
3. Under **Notification preferences**, check **Show the unread count on the bell** and, if email is set up, **Also send me an e-mail for new alerts**. **Quiet hours (no e-mail)** prevent emails at night.
4. Click **Save**.

> **Tip:** marking an alert as read only affects you. An alert disappears for everyone when its problem is fixed (for example when the stock is replenished).

> **Tip:** you only see alerts for your branches. A repeating alert is grouped: "85 times" shows how many times it happened.
