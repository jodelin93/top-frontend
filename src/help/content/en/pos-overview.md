After signing in, sales staff land on the till screen. It has three areas:

- **The top bar**: the store, the till, the shift and the quick-access buttons.
- **Products** (on the left): the search, the categories and the product tiles.
- **The cart** (on the right): the customer, the salesperson, the items, the totals and the **Charge** button.

![The till screen](shot:pos-overview)

A sale always follows the same order:

1. [Add the products](topic:pos-sell) to the cart.
2. [Choose the customer](topic:pos-customer), if needed.
3. [Apply discounts](topic:pos-discounts), if needed.
4. Click **Charge** (F12).
5. [Record the payment or payments](topic:pos-payment), then click **Complete sale**.
6. [Hand over the receipt](topic:pos-receipt) and the change.

## The top bar

| Item | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Store name | Reminds you which store you are working in. |
| Till list | Chooses the till and the branch for this station. The choice is kept on the device. |
| **Open shift** / **SH-000004 · Open** | Opens the till shift, or shows the current shift (see [Open a shift](topic:shifts-open)). |
| **Open drawer** | Opens the drawer without a sale, with a reason. Visible when the shift is open. |
| **Clock in** / **Clock out** | Records your hours. Visible if your account is linked to an employee profile. |
| **Online** / **Offline** | Connection status. A click opens the [offline sales](topic:offline-sync). |
| **Held carts** | The [carts set aside](topic:pos-held-carts) (F9). |
| Keyboard icon | The [keyboard shortcuts](topic:pos-shortcuts). |
| **?** | This help (F1). |
| **Returns** | [Return or exchange](topic:returns-pos). Visible to people allowed to give refunds. |
| **Admin** | Opens the admin area (owner, administrator, manager). |
| Bell | The [notifications](topic:notifications). |
| **EN FR HT ES** | The [language](topic:language) of this device. |
| Shield | [Account security](topic:account-security). |
| Exit arrow | **Sign out**. |

> **Tip:** always sign out before handing the till to a coworker. Otherwise, their sales will be recorded under your name.

> **Tip:** if the store requires it, the till refuses to sell until the shift is open.
