The export buttons appear at the top of the report if you have the **Export reports** permission.

1. Open the report and set the period and branch.
2. Click **CSV**, **Excel** or **PDF**.
3. The file downloads with the same filters as the screen.

> **Tip:** **CSV** opens in any spreadsheet app, **Excel** keeps number formatting, **PDF** is handy for printing or sending to the accountant.

## Export in the background (large reports)

For a long report (a whole year), the file is prepared on the server while you keep working.

1. Choose the format in the small list (**Excel**, **CSV** or **PDF**).
2. Click **Export in background**.
3. At the bottom of the report, **Background exports** shows the task: **Building the file...** then **Ready**.
4. Click **Download**. The trash can removes the export from the list.

> **Warning:** the file stays available for 24 hours (**Available until …**), then becomes **Expired**. Each click on **Download** creates a link valid for 15 minutes: if it has expired, click again.

The list shows your exports from the last 7 days; each person sees only their own. On the **Products** page, **Export CSV** and **Export Excel** export the whole catalog.
