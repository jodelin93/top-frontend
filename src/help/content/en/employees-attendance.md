## Clock in at the till

An employee whose employee record is linked to their account clocks in themselves:

1. Sign in to the till with your account.
2. At the top of the screen, click **Clock in** at the start of the day (on a phone: **⋯** then **Clock in**).
3. The button turns green and shows **Clock out**; hover over it to see **Clocked in since …**.
4. At the end of the day, click **Clock out**.

> **Tip:** the button does not appear if your account is not linked to an active employee record. You can only clock in at a branch you are assigned to.

## Record hours for an employee

1. In **Employees**, open the **Attendance** tab, then click **Record hours**.
2. Choose the **Employee**.
3. Enter the date and time in **Clock in** and **Clock out**.
4. Write the **Reason**, for example "Forgot to clock in".
5. Click **Save**.

> **Warning:** the time cannot be in the future, clock-out must be after clock-in, and a shift cannot be longer than 24 hours. You cannot record a new clock-in for an employee who is already clocked in: close their open clock-in first.

## The attendance report

![The Attendance tab](shot:employees-attendance)

1. In **Attendance**, choose the start date and end date.
2. Choose an employee, or **All employees**.
3. The table shows the **Shifts worked** and the total **Hours**. Click a row for details, and **Hide** to close it.

Each shift shows where it came from (**at the till** or **recorded by a manager**, with the reason). If an employee is still clocked in, **clocked in** appears next to their name; a manager can click **Clock out now**.
