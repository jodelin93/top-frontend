The exchange rate lets customers pay in dollars or gourdes. The **Exchange rates** card is at the top of the **Dashboard** and in the **General** tab of the settings.

![The Exchange rates card](shot:settings-exchange-rate)

1. On the **Exchange rates** card, click **Update rate**.
2. Type the new rate, for example **1 USD = 132.5 HTG**.
3. To accept another currency, click **Another currency** (or **Add a currency** if only one currency is accepted).
4. Click **Save rates** (or **Cancel**).

The new rate applies immediately to new sales. Sales already made keep their rate; a return follows the rate of the original sale.

> **Warning:** check the rate every morning before opening the registers. A wrong rate throws off the change given in HTG.

> **Tip:** **History** shows past rates, with the date and author of each change.
