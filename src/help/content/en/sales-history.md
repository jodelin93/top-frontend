The **Sales** page lists all sales with the number, date, customer, cashier, total and status.

![The sales history](shot:admin-sales)

1. In the menu, click **Sales**.
2. Type a sale number in the search. Sales made offline also have an OFFLINE-… number you can search for.
3. Filter by status: **Completed**, **Voided**, **Payment pending**, **Held**…
4. Choose a period: **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **This month** or **Custom range** (from ≤ to).
5. Use **Previous** and **Next** to change pages.

> **Tip:** a sale can also be **Refunded** or **Partially refunded** after a return. The salesperson's name appears under the cashier when it is different.

## Sale details and reprinting

1. Click the sale's row (on a phone: its card).
2. The full receipt appears: items, taxes, total, payments and change given.
3. To print it again, click **Reprint (copy)**. The printed receipt is marked COPY.

![A sale's details](shot:sale-detail)

From these details, you can also **Return items** ([return](topic:returns-pos)) or **Void sale** ([void](topic:sales-void)).

## Send the receipt to the customer

1. In the sale details, click **Email receipt**.
2. Enter the customer's email address.
3. Check **The customer asked to receive this receipt by e-mail (no marketing)**.
4. Click **Send**.

For SMS or WhatsApp, click **Receipt link (SMS, WhatsApp)**: the app creates a link to the receipt, valid for 30 days.

> **Warning:** this is only for sending the receipt; it does not give the customer's consent to receive marketing. If the address is not that of the sale's customer, their contact details are left off the document sent.
