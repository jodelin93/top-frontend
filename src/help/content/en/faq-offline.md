## The till is offline

The badge at the top changes from **Online** to **Offline**. Don't panic: the till keeps selling for the allowed time (24 hours by default). Sales are kept on the device and then sent automatically when the Internet comes back.

Check the cable, the Wi-Fi or the router. As soon as the connection comes back, leave the till open for a few minutes so it can send its sales. The manager follows the upload on the [Devices](topic:devices) page.

## The till is blocked and refuses to sell offline

The offline selling time has run out, or an offline sales limit has been reached. Reconnect the till to the Internet: the permission renews at once. If the screen says the clock went backward, fix the device's date and time.

If the till really cannot connect anymore, export its sales (**Offline sales** window, **Export unsynced sales**). A manager imports them from the **Devices** page.

## An offline sale is "Needs review"

The server refused the sale and shows the reason (stock, price, shift…). Have a manager fix the cause, then click **Retry** in the **Offline sales** window.
