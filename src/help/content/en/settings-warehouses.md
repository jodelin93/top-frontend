Stock is kept in **locations** (shelf, stockroom, aisle) inside a **warehouse**.

![The Warehouses & locations tab](shot:settings-warehouses)

1. Open the **Warehouses & locations** tab.
2. Click **New warehouse** to add a stockroom or depot.
3. Under **Branch warehouses**, check the warehouses each branch works with, then click **Save** on the row. Only people with access to all branches can change this.
4. Under **Stock locations**, click **New location**. Choose its type (**Zone**, **Aisle**, **Bin**, **Sales floor**, **Transit**) and its stock state (**Sellable**, **Damaged**, **Quarantine**).

> **Tip:** only stock in sellable locations can be sold. Quarantine or damaged-goods locations hold stock that is not sold.
