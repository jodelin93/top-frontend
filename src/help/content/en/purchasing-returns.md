1. Click the **Supplier returns** tab, then **New return**.
2. Choose the **Supplier**, then the original **Receipt**.
3. In the **Return** column, enter the number of units sent back (at most the **Can return** quantity).
4. Enter the **Reason** and, if the supplier gave one, the **Return authorisation (RMA)** number.
5. Click **Record return**.

> **Tip:** the returned units leave the stock of the receiving location. A supplier credit for their value is created automatically and reduces what you owe.
