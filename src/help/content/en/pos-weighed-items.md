Some products are sold by weight, length or volume (for example rice by the kilo).

- **With a scale label**: scan the label. The weight or price is read from the barcode and the line is added automatically.
- **Without a label**: click the product. The **Enter the quantity** window opens. Type the weight shown on the scale, for example 1.250, then click **Apply**.

In the cart, the line shows as "1.250 kg × US$1.20/kg". To correct the weight, click the line's quantity.

> **Tip:** each scale label creates its own line, even for the same product.

> **Tip:** weighed products and their unit are set up in Admin (see [Units and products sold by weight](topic:products-units-weight)). The label format is set in [Scale labels](topic:settings-weighted-barcodes).
