The app is available in English, French, Haitian Creole and Spanish. Each person chooses their language on their own device; the choice is kept for later sign-ins.

- **Sign-in page and till (computer):** click **EN**, **FR**, **HT** or **ES** at the top right. The active button is blue.
- **Till on a phone:** tap **⋯** (**More actions**), then choose the language in the list.
- **Admin:** at the bottom of the menu, choose the language in the **Language** list.
- **Help center:** the language buttons are at the top of the page.

The change is immediate, including help. The store's default language is set in **Settings** > **General**: it applies to anyone who has not made a personal choice.

> **Tip:** product, category and payment method names stay in the language they were entered in. So you will sometimes see "Cash" or "Card".
