The manager, administrator, and owner track all shifts from **Admin** > **Shifts & cash**.

![The register shift list](shot:admin-shifts)

1. Filter by status (**Open**, **Counting**, **Closed**), by register, or by dates (**From** and **To**: the start date must be before the end date).
2. Check **Variance above tolerance** to see only problem shifts. They have a red triangle.
3. Click a shift to open its details.

A shift's details show the **Opening float**, **Expected**, **Counted**, **Variance** and its reason, the drawer calculation, sales by payment method, and **Cash movements**. From these details, the manager can:

- reprint the **Z-report** (closed shift) or an **X-report** (shift still open);
- view the **Drawer ledger** (each opening, with its reason);
- click **Close shift** or **Force-close** to close the shift of an absent cashier;
- see **Late uploads after close** (offline sales that arrived after closing);
- add a correction (see [Cash cuts, business day, and corrections](topic:shifts-settings)).

> **Warning:** only people allowed to manage all shifts (manager, administrator, owner) can force-close a shift or add a correction.

> **Tip:** on a phone, the list appears as cards; tap a card to open the details.
