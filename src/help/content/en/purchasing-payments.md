## Pay a supplier

1. Click the **Payments** tab, then **New payment**.
2. Choose the **Supplier**.
3. Enter the **Amount**, the **Date** (not in the future) and the **Method** (**Cash**, **Bank transfer**, **Cheque**, **Card**, **Mobile money** or **Other**).
4. Enter the **Reference** (check or transfer number).
5. In **Allocate to invoices**, enter the amount paid for each invoice, or click **Oldest first**.
6. Click **Record payment**.

> **Tip:** a payment can be partial. Anything not allocated stays on account with the supplier and can be allocated later with **Allocate**. **New credit** records a credit note received from the supplier.

## Track payables

The **Payables** tab shows what you owe each supplier, sorted by how late it is: **Not yet due**, **1–30 days**, **31–60 days**, **61–90 days**, **Over 90 days**.

![The Payables tab](shot:purchasing-payables)

1. Change the **Aging as of** date if needed.
2. Click a supplier to open their **Statement**: invoices, credits and payments with the running balance.
3. Click **Print** to print the statement.

> **Tip:** you can pay a supplier in another currency than theirs. Choose the currency next to the **Amount**: gourdes paid against a dollar balance are converted at the sell rate; dollars paid against a gourde balance are converted at the buy rate. The line under the amount shows what it settles.
