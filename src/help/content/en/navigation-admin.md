Admin is where you manage the store: sales, products, stock, staff and settings. Cashiers do not have access to it.

1. On the till screen, click **Admin**.
2. The first page your role allows is shown (usually the **Dashboard**).
3. To go back to the till, click **Back to POS** at the bottom of the menu.

![The Admin menu](shot:admin-sidebar)

## The menu

The left menu is organized into sections: **Sales**, **Catalog**, **Stock**, **People** and **System**. Everyone sees only the pages their role allows. At the top of the menu: your email, your role, the **Help** button and the [notifications](topic:notifications) bell.

At the bottom of the menu, you always find:

- the **Language** list;
- **Help**;
- **Account security**;
- **Back to POS**;
- **Sign out**.

## On a phone

On a narrow screen, the menu is collapsed:

1. Tap the **☰** button (**Open menu**) at the top left.
2. The menu slides in from the left. Tap a page: it closes by itself.
3. To close it without choosing, tap **✕** (**Close menu**), the grayed-out area or the Esc key.

The top bar keeps the page title, the **Help** button and the bell.

![Admin on a phone, menu open](shot:admin-phone-menu)

On a phone, tables are shown as **cards**: one card per row, with the same information and the same buttons as the table. Tap a card to open it.

> **Tip:** the widest pages (reports, inventory) remain usable on a phone, but a computer or tablet is more comfortable for long data entry.
