Use this feature for a delivery without a purchase order. For a supplier order, use [Receive a delivery](topic:purchasing-receive).

1. In the **Stock** tab, click **Receive stock**.
2. Choose the **Location** receiving the goods.
3. Enter the **Supplier reference** (invoice or delivery note) and, if needed, **Notes**.
4. Search for each product in **Products** and click **Add**.
5. Enter the **Good** quantity, the **Damaged** quantity and the **Unit cost**.
6. Click **Receive stock**.

![The Receive stock window](shot:inventory-receive)

> **Tip:** the unit cost you enter updates the product cost (weighted average cost or FIFO, depending on the store's method). Damaged units go to quarantine if the warehouse has one.
