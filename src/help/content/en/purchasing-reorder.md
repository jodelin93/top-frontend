The **Reorder** tab lists the products whose stock is below the **Reorder point**, with the quantity already on order and a suggested quantity.

![The Reorder tab](shot:purchasing-reorder)

1. Choose the location in **Stock at**.
2. Adjust each product's quantity in the **Order** column.
3. Click **Create draft PO**: a draft is created for the preferred supplier.
4. Check it and submit it in the **Purchase orders** tab.

> **Tip:** a product without a supplier appears under **No supplier yet**. Add it to a supplier (the supplier's **Products** button) so you can order it from here.
