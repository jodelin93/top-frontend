1. Open the **Tax rates** tab.
2. Click **New tax rate**.
3. Enter the code, name and rate as a percentage.
4. Save. Then choose the default rate in the **General** tab.

![The Tax rates tab](shot:settings-taxes)

> **Tip:** to tax some products at a different rate (or exempt them), create a [tax category](topic:tax-categories) and assign it to the products.
