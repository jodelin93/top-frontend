## Sell on account

An **On account** sale is charged to the customer's account, and the customer pays later. It is only for customers who have an account and a credit limit.

1. [Choose the customer](topic:pos-customer) before taking payment.
2. In **Payment**, click **On account**.
3. The message "Charged to …'s account. A manager may need to approve it at checkout." appears. Check the amount, then click **Add**.
4. Click **Complete sale**.


> **Warning:** the cashier needs a manager's approval for a sale on account. Above the credit limit, an additional authorization is required. A customer **On credit hold** can no longer buy on account.

> **Tip:** with no customer selected, the **On account** button shows **Add a customer first**.

## Pay with the customer's store credit

Store credit is credit kept by the store, for example after a [return refunded as store credit](topic:returns-pos).

1. Choose the customer.
2. In **Payment**, click **Store credit**: the button shows the available amount.
3. Click **Add**, then complete with another payment if needed.

> **Tip:** the **Store credit** button only appears if the selected customer has store credit. Both methods need an internet connection.

Balances and payments are tracked in [Customer accounts](topic:customers-accounts).
