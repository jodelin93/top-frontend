## Aging

The **Aging** tab groups stock by age (0 to 30 days, 31 to 60 days… over 180 days) with its value. Use it to spot products that don't sell and plan a promotion.

## Valuation

The **Valuation** tab is visible to people who can see costs and can adjust stock or manage settings.

![The Valuation tab](shot:inventory-valuation)

- **Costing method**: **Moving average** or **FIFO** (first in, first out). The **Switch to FIFO...** or **Switch to moving average...** button changes the method.
- **Revalue a product**: corrects a product's unit cost (wrong purchase price). Quantities do not change.

To revalue:

1. Click **Revalue...**, then **Choose** next to the product.
2. Enter the **New unit cost** and the **Reason (required)**.
3. Confirm. The value difference is recorded as a revaluation and logged.

> **Warning:** changing the costing method is an accounting decision. Talk to your accountant first.
