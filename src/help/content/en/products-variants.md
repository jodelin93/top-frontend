Before creating variants, check your attributes (Size, Color…) with the **Attributes** button at the top of the **Products** page. Each attribute has a name, a code and a comma-separated list of options.

## Manage variants

1. On the row of a variable product, click the **Variants** icon (the layers).
2. The **Variants of …** window lists each variant: attributes, SKU, barcode, price, cost, stock and status.
3. To add a variant, click **Add variant**, fill in the row, then confirm.
4. To edit a variant, click its pencil.

## Create several variants at once

1. Click **Generate from attributes**.
2. Click the values you want for each attribute (they turn blue).
3. Enter the **Price of new variants** and, if you know it, the **Unit cost**.
4. Click **Preview** to see the combinations, then **Create variants**.

> **Tip:** each combination of the chosen values becomes a variant; combinations that already exist are skipped.

## Add barcodes to a variant

A variant can have several barcodes (old and new packaging, for example).

1. In the variants window, click the **Barcodes** icon (the vertical bars).
2. Scan or type the code in **Scan or type a barcode**.
3. Click **Add**. The trash can removes a code.

> **Warning:** a barcode can belong to only one variant. Scanning any of its codes finds the variant at the till.
