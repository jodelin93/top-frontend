## Discount on a line

1. On the product line, type the percentage in the **%** box.
2. The discount shows in green under the line total.
3. If the discount is over the store limit, fill in the **Reason for the discount (required)** field.

> **Warning:** above the limit, the cashier sees **Needs a manager’s approval at checkout**. A manager will have to approve when taking payment (see [Get a manager's approval](topic:approvals)).

## Discount on the whole cart or promo code

1. Click **Discount** (**F8** key).
2. For a promo code, type it in **Discount code** and click **Apply**. The accepted code shows with its description.
3. For a free discount, go to **Manual discount on the sale**. Choose **%** or **Amount**, type the value and click **Apply**.
4. Above the store limit, fill in the requested reason.
5. Close the window. The button shows the active discount, for example **WELCOME10**.

![The Discounts window](shot:pos-discount-dialog)

> **Tip:** click **Remove** to take off a code. Promo codes need an internet connection.

> **Warning:** a code whose end date has passed is refused, even if its status is still **Active**.

Automatic discounts (active promotions, the customer's group discount) apply without you doing anything; they appear in the totals and on the receipt.
