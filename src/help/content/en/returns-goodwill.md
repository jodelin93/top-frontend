A goodwill refund gives money back without taking back any goods, for example as a gesture for an unhappy customer.

1. Click **Returns**, find the sale, then click **Goodwill refund**.
2. Type the **Amount to refund (no goods come back)**.
3. Fill in the **Reason for the return**.
4. Choose **Refund to** and the **Register**.
5. Click **Refund …**.

> **Warning:** goodwill refunds are limited to people who have this permission (manager, administrator, owner). Otherwise, the **Manager approval needed** window opens. After a goodwill refund, the sale can no longer be voided.
