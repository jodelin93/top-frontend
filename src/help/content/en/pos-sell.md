You have three ways to add a product:

- **Scan the barcode**: the product is added to the cart automatically.
- **Search**: type part of the name, the SKU or the barcode in **Search products or scan a barcode... (F2)**, then click the product tile.
- **Browse**: click a category (**All**, **Drinks**…) then the product tile.

Each tile shows the name, the variant (size, color), the SKU, the price and the stock, for example **65 in stock**.

Clicking the same tile again (or scanning the product again) increases the quantity on its line: the product appears only once in the cart. The line keeps its discount or changed price. Only items sold by weight get one line per weighing.

## Manage the cart

The cart, on the right, shows each line with its unit price, quantity and total.

- Click **+** or **−** to change the quantity, or type it in the box.
- Click the trash can to remove the line.
- Click **Add a note** to write a remark printed on the receipt.
- Click **Clear cart** to remove everything (you are asked to confirm).

Below the lines: the **Subtotal**, discounts, **Tax**, the **Total** and its equivalent in the other currency (for example **≈ in HTG**).

> **Warning:** the till never sells more than the available stock. An **Out of stock** tile is grayed out. If you ask for too many, a message shows the available quantity, for example "Only 3 × … in stock".

> **Tip:** if a scanned code is unknown, the message "No product found for …" appears. Check the item or search for it by name.

> **Tip:** on the keyboard, **↑ ↓** select a line, **+** and **−** change its quantity and **Delete** removes it.

Offline, searching by name, SKU or barcode also works: it uses the copy of the catalog kept on the device (see [Sell offline](topic:offline-mode)).
