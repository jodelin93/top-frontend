If a customer asks for their personal data to be erased, you can anonymise them.

1. Open the customer profile.
2. At the bottom of the profile, click **Anonymise**.
3. Read the confirmation: the name, contact details, addresses and notes will be permanently erased.
4. Confirm with **OK**, or click **Cancel** to do nothing.

The profile becomes inactive and shows **This customer was anonymised on …**. Sales and account history are kept for accounting.

> **Warning:** anonymisation is permanent. It is refused while the customer owes money or still has a balance in their favor. You need the permission to manage customers.
