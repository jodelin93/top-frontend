Several settings offer a **Takes effect** field: the change applies automatically at the chosen date and time.

> **Warning:** the effective time must be in the future, or empty to apply right away ("Choose a time in the future, or leave empty").

1. Open the **History** tab.
2. Each row shows the **Version**, the **Status** (**applied**, **scheduled** or **cancelled**), **Effective**, the **Changed** settings and the author (**By**).
3. Click **View** to see the details of a version.
4. To drop a **scheduled** change, click **Cancel** on its row.
