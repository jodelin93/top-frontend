The **Admin** > **Customers** page is reserved for people who can edit customers (owner, administrator, manager). Cashiers search for and add customers from the till. The tabs are **Customers**, **Possible duplicates**, **Groups** and **Custom fields**.

![The customer list](shot:admin-customers)

## Search

1. Type a name, code, email or phone number in the search box.
2. If needed, filter by status (**Active**, **Inactive**, **Blocked**) or by group.
3. The table shows the group, the **Points** and the **Last purchase** date.
4. Click the eye to open the [customer profile](topic:customers-profile), or the pencil to edit it.

## Create a customer

1. Click **New customer**.
2. Choose the **Type**: **Individual** or **Company**. Leave the **Code** empty: it is created automatically.
3. Enter the **First name**, **Last name** (or the company name), **Email** and **Phone**. The date of birth is optional and cannot be in the future or before 1900.
4. For a customer who buys on credit, enter the **Credit limit** and the **Payment terms (days)**.
5. Choose the customer's **Group** (its group prices will apply at the till).
6. Under **Marketing consent**, check **Agrees to marketing emails** and/or **Agrees to marketing SMS** only if the customer agrees, then fill in **How was consent given?**.
7. Click **Create customer**.

![The New customer form](shot:customer-form)

> **Warning:** never send an advertising message to a customer who has not given consent. Every consent change is dated and kept in the consent **History**.

> **Tip:** if a customer with the same email or phone number already exists, the app warns you before creating it.
