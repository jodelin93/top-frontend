This information is printed on every receipt and every invoice.

![The Business information tab](shot:settings-business)

1. Open the **Business information** tab.
2. In **Store logo**, upload a JPEG, PNG or WebP image of 2 MB or less.
3. Fill in the **Store (trading) name**, the **Legal / company name**, the **Tax ID (NIF / VAT / TIN)** and the **Business registration no.**
4. Complete the address, phone, email and website.
5. Write your **Return policy**.
6. Click **Save changes**.

> **Tip:** a black-and-white logo prints better on thermal paper. Leave the address empty to use each branch's address.
