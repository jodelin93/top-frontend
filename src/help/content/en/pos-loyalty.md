The chosen customer must have points. The **Loyalty points** button shows their points and their value, for example "4037 pts = $40.37".

1. [Choose the customer](topic:pos-customer) before charging.
2. In **Payment**, click **Loyalty points**.
3. Type the amount to pay with points, then click **Add**.
4. Add another payment if needed.

> **Warning:** the store sets a minimum number of points per use (100 for example) and a maximum share of the sale that can be paid with points (50% for example). Points need a connection. With no customer, the message "Add a customer to use points" appears.

> **Tip:** the customer earns points on the items bought. The part paid with points, a gift card, store credit or on account does not earn points. The receipt shows the **Points earned** and the **Points balance**.

The rules are set in [Loyalty program](topic:settings-loyalty); balance corrections are made on the [customer's profile](topic:customers-loyalty).
