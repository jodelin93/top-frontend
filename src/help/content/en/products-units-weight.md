To sell rice, sugar, fabric or fuel, first create a unit with decimals.

1. On the **Products** page, click **Units**.
2. Click **New unit**.
3. Enter the **Name** (e.g. Kilogram) and the **Code** (e.g. kg).
4. Check **Allows decimal quantities (e.g. 1.25 kg)** and choose the number of **Decimal places**.
5. Click **Save unit**.

Then, in the product form:

1. Choose this unit in **Unit of measure**. The price you enter becomes the price per unit (per kilo, per meter…).
2. If a scale prints labels, enter its item code in **PLU (scale code)**.
3. Save the product.

> **Tip:** at the till, the cashier enters the weighed quantity (e.g. 1.250 kg). Scale labels (barcodes starting with 20 to 29) are read automatically if they are set up (see [Scale labels](topic:settings-weighted-barcodes)).
