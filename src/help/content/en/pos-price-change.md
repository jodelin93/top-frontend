The price comes from the catalog. Only change it in specific cases, for example a damaged item.

1. In the cart, click the line's unit price (the text with the small pencil).
2. Type the **New unit price**.
3. Click **Apply**. The old price appears crossed out on the line.
4. To go back to the normal price, click **Use catalog price**.

![The Change price window](shot:pos-price-dialog)

> **Warning:** a cashier is not allowed to change a price alone. A manager will have to approve at checkout (see [Get a manager's approval](topic:approvals)). Offline, this approval is not possible.
