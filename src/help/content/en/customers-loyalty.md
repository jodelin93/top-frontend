Customers earn points on every purchase and can use them to pay at the till. The rules are set in [Loyalty program](topic:settings-loyalty).

To correct a balance (mistake, goodwill gesture):

1. Open the customer's profile, **Details** tab, **Loyalty points** section.
2. Enter the number of points to add (e.g. 50) or remove (e.g. -50).
3. Enter the **Reason (required)**.
4. Click **Adjust**.

> **Warning:** points are worth money. Only people who can adjust customer accounts (owner, administrator, manager) can change them; for everyone else, the **Manager approval needed** window opens. Every adjustment is logged.
