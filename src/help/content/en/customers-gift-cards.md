- A **Gift card** is sold at the till (see [Sell and use a gift card](topic:pos-gift-cards)).
- **Store credit** is created, for example, when a return is refunded as store credit; the customer uses it as a payment method.

To view or correct a card or store credit:

1. In the menu, click **Customer accounts**, then the **Gift cards & store credit** tab.
2. Filter by type or type the last 4 characters of the card.
3. Click **Adjust** on the row you want.
4. Enter the amount (+25.00 to add, -10.00 to remove) and the **Reason**.
5. Click **Adjust**.

![The Gift cards & store credit tab](shot:customer-gift-cards)

> **Warning:** adding more than 500 to a gift card or store credit needs a second person's approval, even if you have the permission: the **Manager approval needed** window opens.

> **Tip:** cards expire after the number of months set in [General settings](topic:settings-general) (**Gift cards expire after (months)**).
