Groups (Retail, VIP, Wholesale…) bring together customers who get the same terms.

![The Groups tab](shot:customer-groups)

1. In **Customers**, click the **Groups** tab, then **New group**.
2. Enter the **Code** and **Name**.
3. Choose a **Default price list** and a **Discount %** if needed.
4. Enter the **Payment terms (days)** for the group's on-account sales.
5. Click **Create group**.
6. To put a customer in the group, edit their profile and choose the **Group**.

> **Warning:** as soon as a customer in the group is chosen at the till, the group's price list and discount apply automatically, with no approval. The discount shows on the cart and on the receipt as "Group discount". Removing the customer brings back the normal prices (see [Group prices and discount](topic:pos-group-pricing)).
