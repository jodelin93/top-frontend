Each user is given a role. The role decides which menus are visible and which actions are allowed. Roles can be viewed in **Admin** > **Roles & permissions** (owner and administrator).

| Role | For whom | What it can do | What it cannot do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Owner** | The store's boss | Everything, including managing other owners. This role cannot be changed. | — |
| **Admin** | The owner's right hand | Manage everything: sales, stock, settings, staff accounts, roles, devices. | Edit or remove an owner. |
| **Manager** | The day-to-day store manager | Sell, void, refund, manage all shifts, approve variances, discounts and prices, manage products, stock, purchasing, customers, reports, settings. | Manage staff accounts, roles, devices and system monitoring. |
| **Cashier** | Till staff | Sell, give discounts up to the store limit, put sales on hold, open and close their own shift, record expenses, find and create customers, make estimates, view stock, reprint a receipt. | Change a price, go over the discount limit, sell on credit, make cash movements, refund, void. These actions need [a manager's approval](topic:approvals). |
| **Inventory clerk** | The stock person | Receive, adjust, count and transfer stock, manage suppliers, purchase orders and products. | Sell: no access to the till. |
| **Accountant** | The accountant | Dashboard, reports and exports, sales (read only), expenses, card settlements, audit log. | Sell or edit sales. |

> **Tip:** when a cashier tries a restricted action, the **Manager approval needed** window opens. A manager enters their credentials on the spot; the approval is valid only once, for that action.

> **Tip:** if a page is missing from your menu, nothing is broken: your role does not give access to it. Ask the administrator.
