A customer with credit can buy "on account" and pay later, within their credit limit. The **Customer accounts** page requires the permission to view balances.

![Customer balances by aging](shot:customer-accounts)

## View all balances

1. In the menu, click **Customer accounts**.
2. The **Aging** tab lists customers who owe money, sorted by how late they are.
3. Choose **With a balance** or **All accounts**.

> **Tip:** follow up first on the 61–90 days and 90+ days columns.

## A customer's account

1. Open the customer's profile, **Account** tab.
2. Read the **Balance**, **Credit limit**, **Available credit**, and **Payment terms**.
3. The aging table shows **Current**, then amounts late by **1–30 days**, **31–60 days**, and so on.
4. **Account entries** lists each sale on account and each payment.

> **Tip:** check **Credit hold (no new sales on account)** on the profile of a customer who doesn't pay.

## Take a payment on the account

1. In the **Account** tab, click **Receive payment**.
2. Enter the **Amount**, choose **Paid with** and, if needed, a **Reference**.
3. For cash, choose the **Register**: the payment goes into its open shift.
4. Click **Record payment**.

> **Warning:** a non-cash payment over 500 that clears a debt requires approval from a second person. **Adjust balance** (+ the customer owes more, − they owe less, reason required) is reserved for the manager or administrator.

## Print a statement

1. In **Account**, **Statement** section, choose the **From** and **To** dates (the start date cannot be after the end date).
2. Click **Show statement**: **Opening balance**, transactions, **Closing balance**.
3. Click **Print** (or save as PDF) and give it to the customer.

> **Tip:** a customer can pay their account in gourdes. In **Receive payment**, choose **HTG** next to the amount and type the amount in gourdes: the app shows its dollar value at the sell rate, and that is what comes off the balance. In cash, the gourdes go into the gourdes in the drawer.
