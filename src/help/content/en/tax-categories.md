Tax categories let you tax certain products at a different rate (food, tax-exempt products…).

1. In the menu, click **Price lists & tax**.
2. Scroll down to **Tax categories** and click **New tax category**.
3. Enter the **Code** and **Name**, and choose the **Tax rate**. Choose **None (tax exempt)** for a product with no tax.
4. Save, then assign this category to the relevant products in their product details.

> **Tip:** a product with no tax category uses the store's default tax rate. The rates themselves are set in [Tax rates](topic:settings-taxes).
