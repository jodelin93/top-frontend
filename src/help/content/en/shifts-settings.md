Below the list of shifts, the **Shifts & cash** page has two settings and, in each shift's details, the corrections.

## Denominations

The **Cash denominations** section lists the bills and coins offered for counting (opening and closing).

1. Add, edit or remove the values for each currency.
2. Click **Save**. **Use defaults** restores the original list.

## Business day

The **Business day** section sets the time the day starts (**Day starts at**). With 04:00, a sale made at 02:30 counts toward the previous day in reports. This setting requires the right to manage settings.

## Correct a closed shift

A closed shift is never reopened: you correct it with a reason.

1. Open the shift from the list.
2. In **Corrections after close**, click **Add correction**.
3. Choose **Expected cash correction** or **Counted cash correction**, enter the amount and the reason.
4. Click **Save correction**.

The correction is added to the shift's history and to the audit log; the original figures remain visible.
