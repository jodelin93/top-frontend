The **Settings** page needs the **Manage store settings** permission (owner, administrator, manager). Its tabs: **General**, **Business information**, **Receipts & printing**, **Loyalty**, **Tax rates**, **Payment methods**, **Branches & registers**, **Warehouses & locations**, **Offline & security** and **History**.

![The General tab](shot:settings-general)

1. In the **General** tab, check the **Store name** and the main **Currency** (3-letter code).
2. Choose the **Default tax rate**, applied to every sale.
3. Set the **Low-stock threshold** and the store's default **Language**.
4. Enter the number of months in **Gift cards expire after (months)**.
5. Check **Prices include tax** if the displayed prices already include tax, and **Allow sales with a total of 0.00** if needed.
6. Click **Save settings**.

Further down in the same tab:

- the **Inventory & purchasing** block: approval thresholds (purchase orders, stock counts), tolerances and **Transfer settings**;
- the [Scale labels](topic:settings-weighted-barcodes);
- the [Exchange rate](topic:settings-exchange-rate) card.

> **Tip:** if the **Finish setting up your store** banner appears, click **Create default setup** to set up a branch, a register and payment methods.

> **Tip:** every change is recorded in the [History](topic:settings-history) tab, with the date and who made it.
