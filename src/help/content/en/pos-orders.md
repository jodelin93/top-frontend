For a service you prepare (printing, a banner, a repair...), the customer can pay part now and the rest when they come back. Orders are for items **not tracked in stock** (services).

## Take the order

1. Add the services to the cart as for a sale. Put the details in the line note (size, colours, text).
2. Click **Take as an order** (under the cart).
3. Choose the **Customer**: they are the one who comes back for the order.
4. Choose the **Ready by** date and type the **Notes for the preparation** if needed.
5. Under **Deposit (optional)**, type the amount the customer gives and choose how they pay (cash, card, mobile money...). Any amount works, even none.
6. Click **Take the order**.

The order receipt opens. It shows the total, the **Deposits paid** and the **BALANCE DUE AT PICKUP**. Print it and give it to the customer.

> **Note:** the deposit goes into **your cash session today**: cash is added to the drawer (as a paid-in), other methods appear under **Order deposits** in the session. The order is not a sale yet: the sale is counted at pickup.

## Follow the orders

Click **Orders** in the top bar (on a phone: **⋯** then **Orders**). Search by order number, customer name or phone. Open an order to:

- **Add a deposit**: the customer pays another part (never more than the balance).
- **Mark ready**: the order is prepared. Call the customer. **Back in preparation** undoes it.
- **Print**: the order receipt again, with every deposit.

## Hand the order over

1. In **Orders**, open the order and click **Pick up: take the balance**.
2. The order goes into the cart at its prices. The cart shows **Deposits paid** and the **Balance due**.
3. Click **Charge** and take the balance as usual.

The receipt shows the total, a **Deposit paid** line for the deposits and what the customer pays now. The order becomes **Picked up**.

> **Warning:** a pickup needs a connection. The balance is taken in cash or with a payment confirmed at the till.

## Cancel an order

Open the order and click **Cancel the order**. Type the reason, then choose:

- **Give the deposits back**: each deposit goes back the way it was paid (cash in cash, in the currency it was handed over in; a card deposit by card). Cash comes out of your open session. If a way of paying is no longer available, choose another one.
- **Keep the deposits**: the store keeps them.

> **Note:** cancelling needs the permission "Cancel orders and refund or keep their deposits", or a manager's approval. Voiding the pickup sale puts the order back to **Ready** with its deposits.
