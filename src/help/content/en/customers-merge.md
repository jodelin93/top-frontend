1. In **Customers**, click the **Possible duplicates** tab.
2. The app suggests pairs (same email, same phone, or similar name).
3. Click **Review & merge**, choose the profile to keep, then confirm.

![The Possible duplicates tab](shot:customer-duplicates)

> **Tip:** merging keeps a single profile, moves the purchase history, adds up points and balances, and keeps both consent histories. You need the **Merge duplicate customers** permission.
