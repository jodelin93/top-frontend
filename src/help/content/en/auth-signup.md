On some servers, the sign-in page offers **New here? Create a store**. This page creates a new store and makes you its **Owner**.

![Create a store page](shot:auth-signup)

1. On the sign-in page, click **Create a store**.
2. Enter the **Store name**.
3. Enter your **First name**, **Last name**, **Email** and a **Password** (at least 8 characters).
4. Check the main **Currency** (3-letter code, for example USD or HTG).
5. Click **Create store**.

The app sets up a branch, a register and the Cash and Card payment methods for you. You then land in the app, signed in as the owner.

> **Tip:** if you already have an account (in another store), enter the same email and password: the new store is added to your account. If that account uses two-factor authentication, the code is requested. You can then switch from one store to another in **Account security** (see [Invitations and switching stores](topic:account-invitations)).

> **Warning:** if the message "Sign-up is not available on this server. Ask your administrator for an account." appears, store creation is closed. Ask your administrator for an account.

Recommended next steps:

1. Fill in the [General settings](topic:settings-general) and the [Business information](topic:settings-business).
2. Check the [exchange rate](topic:settings-exchange-rate).
3. Create your [products](topic:products-create) or [import them](topic:products-import).
4. [Add your staff](topic:users-invitations).
